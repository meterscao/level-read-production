# Cover Direction

Create a single 5:3 horizontal, text-free Level Read editorial cover. Use a warm off-white paper background, a dominant black-and-white cutout, one large house-color geometric shape, one small compatible accent, subtle print grain, strong negative space, and a clear action relationship.

Use cobalt blue for institutions, regulation, technology, diplomacy, or data; deep green for climate, health, resources, and money; signal red for warning and rupture; aubergine for culture, identity, platforms, and psychology; burnt umber for history, heritage, trade, land, labour, and housing. Do not use logos, lettering, fake text, watermarks, glossy 3D, or generic hands unless human agency is central to the story.

When the article's point is that a striking comparison needs qualification, make that tension visible. Do not make one side overwhelmingly larger or visually victorious if the story says the comparison is not directly fair; use parallel movement, divided conditions, or an unresolved visual relationship instead.
